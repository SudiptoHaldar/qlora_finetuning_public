## What was implemented

- **`config/aws.yaml`**: profile, account id, region, bucket and its four prefixes, both role
  ARNs with their trusted service, the Bedrock inline policy name, the two instance types and
  the quota minimum, the two Nova model ids, the budget name and cap. No credentials, no email.
- **`scripts/aws_check.py`**: seven check functions, each taking one boto3 client and the
  config and returning a PASS or FAIL result with a reason. A guard turns AWS errors into FAIL
  lines and sign-in problems into one `SIGN-IN REQUIRED` line. Exit codes: 0 all pass, 1 a
  check failed, 2 sign-in or config problem. Only `get_`, `list_`, and `describe_` calls.
- **Tests**: every check has a passing case and at least one failing case; config loading,
  the exit codes, the sign-in message, and the read-only property are covered too.

Built ahead of v0.02, which the spec depends on and which does not exist yet. The version is
therefore self-contained: the script has its own YAML loader, only the `network` marker was
registered, ruff runs with default rules, and no `qlora_finetuning/` package was created.
