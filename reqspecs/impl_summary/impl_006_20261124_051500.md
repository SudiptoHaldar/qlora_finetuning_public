## Summary

Two folders under git-ignored `artifacts/release/` can be uploaded as they are:

- **`adapter/`** (82 MB, of which the adapter's weights are 66 MB): arm A1's two adapter files byte for byte, the base model's
  tokenizer, the prompt, the split manifest, the training facts, the invented sample, a
  licence, and a model card.
- **`merged/`** (8.06 GB): the base model with the adapter merged into its weights, saved
  with greedy generation settings, with Qwen's licence, a notice of what was changed, and
  its own card.

Both cards and the package record are committed files under `reports/packaging/`. The
cards are built from templates and the committed records; no figure in them is typed.

### What the machine showed

All on dev, 499 examples, batch size 8, on the laptop GPU:

| Run | What | Right on execution | Replies that differ from R1 |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| recorded | Arm A1 in the SageMaker scoring job | 358 | |
| R1 | The selected adapter, by name | 358 | |
| R2 | The adapter package, from its folder | 358 | 0 |
| R3 | The merged copy, in 4-bit | 361 | 157 |
| R4 | The merged copy, at full precision | 357 | 199 |

- **The package is the adapter**: the same reply on all 499 questions.
- **The merged copy is not withheld**: 3 more right in 4-bit, 1 fewer at full precision;
  the limit is 10 fewer. Its replies often differ from the adapter's in wording and
  seldom in outcome.
- **The code on each card runs**: three runs in an empty directory, each to the sample's
  expected result.
- The merge took 35 seconds on the CPU. The merged copy answers faster than the adapter
  on the 4-bit base (0.44 and 0.37 seconds a question against 0.72).
- `comparison_freeze status`: nothing frozen has changed. `comparison_cost check`: K1 to
  K8 pass and every figure is as before.

## Issues resolved

- **The package's check could not be "the recorded score again".** The recorded run was on
  another GPU. The check is "the same 499 replies as the adapter by name on this machine",
  which passed; the count happened to match the record too.
- **Two runs made in the same second** could not be ordered by their ids. The newest is
  now found by the id's time stamp and then by when the run's record was written.
- **A status that depended on the card being written** left the adapter's card saying the
  merged copy was not published while the licences were unconfirmed. The card now names
  the merged copy unless it is withheld or not built.
- **`+0` in the record's table** for a run with the same count: shown as `0`.
- **The failure categories read badly as one sentence**: they are a table under Results.
