# Gotchas

**Last verified**: 2026-11-20  
**Sources**: the two guides in `reqspecs/0. PLANNING/planning_material/` ("Gemini guide",
"OpenAI guide"), `reqspecs/0. PLANNING/high_level_plan.md` ("plan"), and what was met while
building req_000 v0.05.

Status: **confirmed** means seen in this repository, account, or machine; **corrected** means
a guide said otherwise and the installed libraries were checked (req_000 v0.03, 2026-11-15);
**unverified** means taken from a guide and not yet checked. Details of the installed stack are
in `../tech/hf_training_stack.md`.

## 1. Model and training
