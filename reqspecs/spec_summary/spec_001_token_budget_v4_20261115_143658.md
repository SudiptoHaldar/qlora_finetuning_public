## Summary

Every fit and dev record is counted as the full sequence Path A trains on: the Qwen chat
template over the system and user texts with thinking off, the target, and the end token. The
tokenizer is `Qwen/Qwen3-4B` at a pinned commit, verified by a fingerprint of its vocabulary
and template; no model weights or GPU are used.

The training length becomes 2,048 tokens, because two fit examples exceed the plan's 1,536
and none exceeds 2,048; nothing is ever truncated, and a record over the length is an error.
The reply limit stays 192. Both values live in `config/token_budget.yaml`. The committed
report gives lengths, counts over each threshold, the longest records by id, and the token
volume of one pass over the training data, with six checks; it holds no example text and no
dollar figure.
