## Summary

The cost model prices the ledger's quantities with `config/pricing.yaml` and writes one
committed record, `reports/comparison/cost_model.json` and `.md`. It says what each tuned
model cost to make, what a question costs for each of the six arms, at what volume Path A
or a Nova model is the cheaper, and whether its totals agree with the AWS bill. It calls no
AWS service and starts nothing billed.

The owner took all seven decisions of the spec as recommended. The price of a real-time
endpoint, read from the public price list on 2026-11-23, is $1.408 an hour for
`ml.g5.xlarge`, the same as the training rate. Nova Pro's training charge is entered from
the bill: $58.75.

The bill as the owner read it on 2026-11-23 (Bedrock $78.19, SageMaker $8.24) equals the
ledger through 2026-11-22 to the cent. The test runs of 2026-11-23 were not on it yet.
